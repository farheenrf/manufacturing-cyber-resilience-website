Why manufacturing?

Smaller manufacturers often rely on mixed office and production technology. Network separation, access control, and recovery planning can reduce unnecessary connectivity and help manage operational risk when designed with production safety and availability in mind.

Scope: This project currently uses educational simulations. It does not claim field deployment, independent validation, or protection against real-world attacks.
