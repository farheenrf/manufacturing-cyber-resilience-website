Transparent methodology

Research and validation

Working objective: Develop and evaluate an accessible, cost-conscious network segmentation and cyber resilience framework for small U.S. manufacturing businesses.

Research questions

Connectivity

What communication exists before segmentation, and how does it change after configuration?

Access control

Can necessary traffic be permitted while unnecessary paths are denied?

Recoverability

Can configurations be restored reproducibly, and how long does recovery take?

Practicality

What hardware, support, operational, and cost constraints affect adoption?
