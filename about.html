About the initiative

Practical, accessible manufacturing cybersecurity

Manufacturing Cyber Resilience is an independent educational and research initiative focused on exploring cost-conscious network segmentation, access control, and recovery practices for smaller U.S. manufacturers.

How the pieces fit together

Research website

Hosts tools, methodology, documentation, limitations, and feedback pathways.

TechTalk Labs

The educational video series that walks through simulated configurations and experiments.

GitHub

Houses public research artifacts and reproducible lab materials.

Our principles

Practicality, transparency, operational safety, reproducibility, affordability, and willingness to incorporate independent critique.
